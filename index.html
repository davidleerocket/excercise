Strength · Mobility · Consistency

Morning Exercise Routine

~19 minutesMon · Wed · FriAny suitable handheld weight

Tap play for exercise demonstrations
01

Warm-up

3 min
  • Forward arm circles30 sec
  • Backward arm circles30 sec
  • Standing torso rotations30 sec
  • Standing cross-body knee raises30 sec
  • Cat-cow stretch60 sec

Loosen up and get blood flowing without tiring yourself out.

02

Strength training

12 min

2 rounds · Rest 30–45 sec between exercises

  • Standard push-ups8–15 reps12
  • Pike push-ups5–10 reps
  • Bent-over rows10–15 reps
  • Biceps curls10–15 reps

Slow, controlled reps; good form. Leave 1–2 reps in reserve.

03

Stretching

4 min
  • Child’s pose60 sec
  • Downward-facing dog60 sec
  • Doorway chest stretch30 sec / side
  • Overhead triceps stretch30 sec / side

Breathe deeply and relax. Gentle tension, never pain.

Keep it simple

Progression: Gradually increase reps, weight or difficulty as you get stronger.

Recovery: Rest at least one day between strength sessions. Stretching on rest days is optional.

Goal: Build upper-body muscle, strength and mobility. Finish energized and challenged, not exhausted.

Consistency over perfection. Stick with it for three months.
